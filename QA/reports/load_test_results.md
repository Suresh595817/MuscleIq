# Baseline Load Test Report

**Target Environment:** Localhost (Vite Dev Server)
**Test Duration:** 61.90 seconds
**Virtual Users:** 100 concurrent connections

---

## What you will see

### Requests per second (RPS)

**262 req/sec**

*Meaning your application successfully handled about 262 requests every single second during the 1-minute sustained load.*

---

### Response Time

- **Fastest Response (Min):** 6.30ms
- **Average Response:** 160.70ms
- **Slowest Response (Max):** 380.66ms

*Meaning:*
- *The quickest a user got their page loaded was a lightning-fast 6.3 milliseconds.*
- *The average wait time under heavy load was still well under a quarter of a second (160ms).*
- *Even in the absolute worst-case scenario (the slowest request out of the thousands sent), a user only waited about 380 milliseconds (less than half a second) for the page to load.*
